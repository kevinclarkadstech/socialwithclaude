This policy explains what [COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us") collects when you use {{appName}}, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

**What you give us**

- **Account:** your email address, the month and year you were born (to check you're old enough; it isn't shown to anyone), and the version of these terms you agreed to and when.
- **Profile:** your username, first and last name if you add them, an about text, and a profile and cover photo if you add them.
- **What you post and send:** posts, listings (with their title, description, condition and price), comments, photos, and chat messages.
- **What you do in the app:** who you follow and block, what you save, how you vote on comments, mentions, and your settings (such as whether your profile is public or private).
- **Reports and feedback:** reports you make, and feedback you send, including the page you sent it from and your device's browser or app version.

**What we collect automatically**

- **Sign-in sessions:** for each device you're signed in on, its IP address and browser or app version, so you can see and sign out of them in Settings.
- **Security and abuse prevention:** we count requests by IP address, and by a scrambled (hashed) form of your email address when you ask for a code, to stop abuse. These counts are kept for at most a day.
- **Logs and error reports:** our servers log requests (the address requested, the result, timing, and your account ID if you're signed in). When something goes wrong, an error report with the same details is sent to our error tracker. These don't include your messages, posts or passwords.

**Photos.** When you upload a photo we remove its location and other hidden metadata (EXIF) before it's published.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising. [If ads are added later, update this section.]

## How we use it

To run {{appName}}: show your profile and content to the people you've chosen, deliver messages, send sign-in codes and notifications, keep your account secure, prevent spam and abuse, review reports and enforce our [Terms of Service](/terms), fix problems, and meet legal duties.

## Who can see what

- **Public profiles** and their posts can be seen by anyone, including people who aren't signed in, and may appear in search engines.
- **Private profiles:** only approved followers see your posts.
- **Photos** are served from public addresses: anyone who has a photo's link can view it, even if your profile is private.
- **Chats** are seen by the people in them, and by our moderators when a message is reported.
- **Moderators** can see reported content and the records of what was done about it.

## How long we keep it

- **While your account exists**, we keep your information until you delete it or your account.
- **Deleting your account:** it's hidden straight away and deleted for good after 30 days (unless you restore it by signing in). That deletes your profile, photos, posts and listings (with every comment on them), comments, votes, saves, follows, blocks, chats (for both people) and notifications.
- **What we keep after that:** reports, with a copy of what was reported, and the record of moderation actions, to protect people, handle appeals and meet legal duties. If you were banned, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your email address so it can't be used to sign up again.

## Children

{{appName}} isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Sign-up asks for your birth month and year; if you're under 13, the account is deleted straight away with what was entered. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, hashed one-time codes, limits on repeated attempts, and access only for those who need it. No service is perfectly secure, so keep your email account secure too.
