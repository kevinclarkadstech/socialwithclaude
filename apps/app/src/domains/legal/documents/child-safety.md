# Child Safety Standards

**Last updated November 18, 2026**

> **Draft.** Everything in [square brackets] is a placeholder. Have it reviewed before launch.

{{appName}} has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)**, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything on {{appName}}: profiles, posts, listings, comments, photos and chat messages.

## What's not allowed

- Any content that sexually exploits or abuses children, including CSAM, whether real, drawn or generated.
- Sexualizing children in any way, including comments, captions and messages.
- Grooming: contacting a child to build trust for sexual purposes, or asking a child for sexual images or to meet.
- Sextortion: threatening to share intimate images of a child, or of anyone, to get more images, money or anything else.
- Trafficking children, or advertising or soliciting children for sexual purposes, including through Marketplace listings.
- Sharing, asking for, or linking to any of the above.

## How to report

- **In the app:** on any post, comment, profile or chat message, open its menu, choose **Report**, then **Child safety**. You can also block the person.
- **By email:** {{childSafetyEmail}}.
- **If a child is in immediate danger,** contact your local police or emergency services first.
- In the US, you can also report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org). Elsewhere, see [INHOPE](https://www.inhope.org) for your country's hotline.

## What we do

- **Review reports** about child safety first, and aim to act within [24 hours].
- **Remove** the content, and **ban** the accounts involved. A banned person can't sign up again with the same email address.
- **Report** apparent CSAM to NCMEC's CyberTipline, as US law requires, and to the relevant authorities elsewhere where the law requires it. We keep what's needed for the report, and cooperate with law enforcement.
- **Scan published photos** against known CSAM (Cloudflare's CSAM Scanning Tool), and act on every match.
- **Strip location data** from photos when they're published.
- Keep {{appName}} for people aged 13 and over: sign-up asks for a birth month and year, and refuses anyone younger. We delete accounts we find belong to younger children.

## Following the law

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including reporting CSAM to NCMEC under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A in the US, [and other jurisdictions where {{appName}} is available].
